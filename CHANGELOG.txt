KIM ALLEN - AURORA DIGITAL CARD - CHANGELOG
Versioning scheme: v#.#.#-[stage] (semver)
Patch = fixes | Minor = new content (patch resets) | Major 0 until launch (v1.0.0)
Stages: -alpha -> -beta at feature-complete -> -rc1 when launch-ready -> v1.0.0 gold
The live version lives in <meta name="version"> in index.html and here, not in the
folder name (renaming the root breaks the Firebase and Actions paths).
Archived copies of outgoing versions live in _version_archive/, never in the working tree.
