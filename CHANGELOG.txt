KIM ALLEN - AURORA DIGITAL CARD - CHANGELOG
Versioning scheme: v#.#.#-[stage] (semver)
Patch = fixes | Minor = new content (patch resets) | Major 0 until launch (v1.0.0)
Stages: -alpha -> -beta at feature-complete -> -rc1 when launch-ready -> v1.0.0 gold
The live version lives in <meta name="version"> in index.html and here, not in the
folder name (renaming the root breaks the Firebase and Actions paths).
Archived copies of outgoing versions live in _version_archive/, never in the working tree.

v0.2.0-alpha (2026-09-06)
- Promos updated to Aurora's current schedule, matching the demo site build: 3X Points All Day
  (Mon), Loyalty Rewards Day (Tue), Win-It Wednesdays (Wed), VIP Rewards Day (Thu) and
  Nifty-Fifty Fridays (Fri, drawings every 30 min 7-9 PM). The old seven-day schedule is gone.
- Saturday and Sunday now carry no promo. WEEK holds null on those days, the grid renders five
  tiles instead of seven, and on a weekend the featured panel looks ahead to Monday and relabels
  itself "Next promo" instead of "Today's featured promo".
- draws is now minutes past midnight rather than whole hours, so the Friday half-hour drawings
  count down correctly. Countdown only runs on the day itself.
- VENUE.promoSub reworded to "Five promos, Monday through Friday. Today's is at the top."
  (archived: _version_archive/card_v0.1.0-alpha/index.html)
